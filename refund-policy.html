Customer care

Return & Refund Policy

At Nana's Mama, we take pride in the quality of our products. If you are not completely satisfied with your purchase, we're here to help.

Returns (Skincare Products)

We accept returns on unused and unopened skincare products, including shea butter and African Black Soap, within 30 days of delivery.

To be eligible for a return:

  • The item must be unused and in its original condition.
  • The item must be in its original packaging.
  • You must provide proof of purchase.

Once we receive and inspect the returned item, we will notify you of the approval or rejection of your refund.

Food & Snack Items (Final Sale)

Due to health and hygiene reasons, all food and snack items are final sale and cannot be returned or refunded.

Damaged or Incorrect Orders

If your order arrives damaged, defective, or incorrect:

  • Contact us within 48 hours of delivery.
  • Email us at nanasmamashea@gmail.com.
  • Include clear photos of the item and packaging.

We will review the issue and offer a replacement or refund where appropriate.

Refunds

If approved:

  • Refunds will be processed to your original payment method.
  • Please allow 5-10 business days for the refund to appear, depending on your bank or payment provider.

Shipping costs are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error.

Return Shipping

Customers are responsible for return shipping costs unless the item was defective or incorrect.

We recommend using a trackable shipping service, as we are not responsible for items lost during return transit.

International Orders

For international returns:

  • Customers are responsible for all return shipping costs.
  • Any customs duties or import fees are non-refundable.

How to Start a Return

To begin a return request, please contact nanasmamashea@gmail.com.

Include:

  • Your order number.
  • The reason for return.
  • Photos, if applicable.

We will respond within 1-2 business days.

Disclaimer

Due to the natural nature of our products, variations in texture, color, or scent may occur. These differences do not qualify as defects.

Still Have Questions?

Email us at nanasmamashea@gmail.com. We're happy to help.
