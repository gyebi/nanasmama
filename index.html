Rooted in heritage

Our Story

Nana’s Mama was born from a mother’s desire to care for her family using natural, time-honored African remedies.

When modern products failed to provide gentle, lasting results, she turned back to traditional shea butter using the same methods passed down through generations.

What began as a personal solution became a mission to share pure, handcrafted skincare rooted in Ghanaian heritage with families everywhere.
